# Projects Showcased on jcsosia.github.io

## HatSwap
**Description:** Minimalist Android app launcher that swaps apps based on your schedule.

**Features:**
- Schedule-based app switching
- Custom hats for different contexts
- Widget integration
- Manual override with Hotswap
- Priority-based conflict resolution
- Local backup and restore

## Quick Contact Sheet
**Description:** Fast, lightweight Android app and Glance home-screen widgets for instant calling, messaging, and reusable preset text snippets.

**Features:**
- Glance home-screen widgets in multiple responsive sizes
- One-tap calling and messaging via native system intents
- Reusable pre-composed message bubbles with drag-and-drop reordering
- Automatic per-contact preset caching
- Local JSON backup and restore
- Custom contact photos and default phone number selection
- 100% offline, privacy-first architecture with zero network permissions

## WaveBars
**Description:** Free, browser-based tool that transforms short audio clips into animated waveform videos for social posts, podcasts, and Canva.

**Features:**
- Real-time animated waveform rendering
- Customizable color presets and theme styling
- Drag-and-drop audio loading
- Direct .webm / .mp4 video export
- 100% in-browser processing with zero account requirement
- Designed for social media clips, podcast promos, and Canva templates
