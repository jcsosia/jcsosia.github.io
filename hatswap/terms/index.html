1. Overview of the Service

HatSwap is a minimalist Android launcher companion and productivity tool designed to help you maintain digital focus. HatSwap allows you to:

  • Group installed apps into context-based profiles called "Hats" (e.g., Work, Personal, Reading).
  • Schedule automated transitions between Hats based on the time of day and days of the week.
  • Access your selected apps directly via home screen widgets.
  • Temporarily override active schedules using our on-demand toggle feature ("Hotswap").
  • Export and import your Hat setups locally via JSON backups.
