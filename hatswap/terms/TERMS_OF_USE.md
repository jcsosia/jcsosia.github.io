## 1. Overview of the Service

HatSwap is a minimalist Android launcher companion and productivity tool designed to help you maintain digital focus. HatSwap allows you to:
- Group installed apps into context-based profiles called **"Hats"** (e.g., Work, Personal, Reading).
- Schedule automated transitions between Hats based on the time of day and days of the week.
- Access your selected apps directly via home screen widgets.
- Temporarily override active schedules using our on-demand toggle feature (**"Hotswap"**).
- Export and import your Hat setups locally via JSON backups.

## 6. Device Permissions, Background Tasks & OS Limitations

### 6.1 System Permissions
To provide its core scheduling and widget features, HatSwap requests standard Android system permissions, including:
- **Package Visibility / Query All Packages:** Used solely to let you choose which installed apps belong to each Hat.
- **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`):** Used to trigger scheduled Hat transitions at exact user-configured times.
- **Battery Optimization Exemption:** The App may prompt you to disable aggressive battery optimization ("Unrestricted" battery setting) to ensure timely background swaps during Android Doze mode.

### 6.2 Operating System & Manufacturer Caveats
Android devices are produced by many manufacturers running customized versions of Android. Certain manufacturers or operating system versions enforce aggressive background task management, deep sleep modes, or customized widget refresh cycles that may occasionally delay or suppress scheduled swaps or widget updates. 

While HatSwap is built using modern Android best practices (Jetpack Glance, Room, WorkManager, AlarmManager), we cannot guarantee that scheduled swaps or widget refreshes will execute instantaneously or without interruption on every device model or custom OS environment.
