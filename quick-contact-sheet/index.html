Android App & Glance Widgets

One tap to reach the people who matter most.

Quick Contact Sheet puts instant calling, texting, and reusable message templates right on your Android home screen. Built with Jetpack Compose, Glance, and Material 3—strictly local, lightweight, and 100% offline.

What it does

Quick Contact Sheet creates one-tap home-screen widgets for your VIP contacts. Tap to call, text, or dispatch pre-written message templates without typing.

100% Private & Offline

Operates entirely on your device with zero network permissions, no telemetry, and no accounts. Uses native Android intents (ACTION_DIAL and ACTION_SENDTO) to route communications securely through your phone's native dialer and messaging app.

Real Experience

Designed for speed, clarity, and one-hand use.

Every interaction is built to get you in and out fast—whether you need a quick call, a standard check-in text, or custom message presets.

Instant Quick Actions

Tap your contact's widget to open the bottom sheet. Start a phone call, open a messaging thread, or tap any pre-composed message snippet to open SMS with text already filled in.

Glance Home-Screen Widget

Clean, responsive Glance widgets that live right on your home screen. Displays your contact's photo and direct action shortcuts in a pill-shaped layout that blends seamlessly into your launcher.

Drag-and-Drop Reusable Snippets

Compose frequently used text snippets like “On my way!” or “Call you in 5”. Drag and drop to rearrange your top favorites for immediate reach.

Per-Widget Personalization

Choose contacts with instant search, select preferred default phone numbers for contacts with multiple lines, and customize avatar photos directly.

Features

Everything Quick Contact Sheet is built to support.

  • Glance home-screen widgets in multiple responsive sizes
  • One-tap calling & texting via native system intents
  • Pre-composed text snippets with 1-tap pre-fill
  • Drag-and-drop message reordering
  • Automatic per-contact preset caching
  • Local JSON backup and restore
  • Custom photo & default number resolution
  • 100% private, offline, and tracker-free
