Agreement & Policies

1. Description of the Application

Quick Contact Sheet is an Android utility providing home-screen widgets (built with Jetpack Glance) and in-app configuration tools designed for fast, one-tap access to your contacts, communication shortcuts, and reusable text snippets.

  • Local & Offline Architecture: Quick Contact Sheet operates strictly offline and locally on your device. It does not require an account, does not host or transmit personal data, and does not request the Android INTERNET permission.
  • System Intent Routing: Quick Contact Sheet acts as a launcher. Calling and texting actions delegate to your device's native dialer (android.intent.action.DIAL) or SMS app (android.intent.action.SENDTO).
